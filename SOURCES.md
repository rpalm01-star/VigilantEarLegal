## Models

The machine-learning models in Vigilant Ear, who made them, and the licenses they are used under. They all run on the phone.

1. **YAMNet** (Recognizing sounds) — Google. Apache License 2.0 (text below). Copyright 2019 The TensorFlow Authors. Changes: Converted to Core ML for iOS. iOS, Android. https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet
2. **PANNs MobileNetV2 (MobileNetV2\_mAP=0.383)** (A second opinion on sounds) — Qiuqiang Kong, Yin Cao, Turab Iqbal, Yuxuan Wang, Wenwu Wang and Mark Plumbley. Pretrained model: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, from "PANNs: Large-Scale Pretrained Audio Neural Networks for Audio Pattern Recognition (Pretrained Models)", https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3987831. Code: MIT License (text below), Copyright (c) 2018-2020 Qiuqiang Kong. Changes: Converted from PyTorch to TensorFlow Lite, with its audio front-end inside the model. Android. https://github.com/qiuqiangkong/audioset\_tagging\_cnn
3. **AudioSet ontology** (The names of the sounds the two models above recognize, mapped to Vigilant Ear's sound categories) — Google. CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. iOS, Android. https://github.com/audioset/ontology
4. **Silero VAD** (Telling speech from other sound) — Silero Team. MIT License (text below), Copyright (c) 2020-present Silero Team. Changes: Core ML conversion by FluidInference (MIT), https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/silero-vad-coreml. iOS, Android. https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
5. **VoxLingua107 ECAPA-TDNN** (Recognizing which language is spoken) — SpeechBrain. Apache License 2.0 (text below). Changes: Converted to Core ML for iOS and to ONNX for Android. iOS, Android. https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/lang-id-voxlingua107-ecapa
6. **ReDimNet B2** (Recognizing a returning voice) — ID R&D. MIT License (text below), Copyright (c) 2024 ID R&D, Inc. Changes: Converted to Core ML for iOS and to ONNX for Android. iOS, Android. https://github.com/IDRnD/ReDimNet
7. **Streaming Sortformer v2.1 (diar\_streaming\_sortformer\_4spk-v2.1)** (Telling speakers apart) — NVIDIA. Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License. NVIDIA Open Model License Agreement (text below). Changes: Core ML conversion by FluidInference, 6-bit palettized, https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/diar-streaming-sortformer-coreml. iOS. https://huggingface.co/nvidia/diar\_streaming\_sortformer\_4spk-v2.1
8. **Vosk speech recognition models (12 languages)** (Captions) — Alpha Cephei. Apache License 2.0 (text below). Android. https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
9. **SpeD ParakeetRo 110M TDT-CTC** (Romanian captions) — Gabriel Pirlogeanu, Alexandru-Lucian Georgescu and Horia Cucu (SpeeD, POLITEHNICA Bucharest); ONNX export by AlinClaudiu. Apache License 2.0 (text below). Based on parakeet-tdt\_ctc-110m by NVIDIA, https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt\_ctc-110m, licensed under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changes: Fine-tuned for Romanian by its makers; ONNX metadata added and the graph optimized by Vigilant Ear. Android. https://huggingface.co/gabrielpirlo/Sped\_ParakeetRomanian\_110M\_TDT-CTC
10. **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3** (Captions in European languages) — NVIDIA. CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changes: Exported to ONNX and quantized to int8 by the sherpa-onnx project (k2-fsa). iOS. https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
11. **Omnilingual ASR 300M CTC v2** (Captions in every other language) — Meta. Apache License 2.0 (text below). Copyright 2025 (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates. Changes: Exported to ONNX and quantized to int8 by the sherpa-onnx project (k2-fsa). iOS. https://github.com/facebookresearch/omnilingual-asr
